Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

This page explains what information Nate Hall Insurance ("I," "me") collects through this website and how it is used. I keep it simple: I only collect what you choose to send me, and I use it to help you.

Information you give me

If you fill out the contact form, I receive the details you enter: your name, email, phone number, ZIP code, the products you're interested in, and any comments. Please don't include sensitive health information, Social Security numbers, or policy numbers in the form. We can talk about those details privately.

How I use it

  • To reply to your question or prepare the quote you asked for.
  • To follow up with you about that request.
  • To keep records as required by insurance regulations.

I do not sell or rent your personal information. If you decide to apply for coverage, your information will be shared with the insurance company you choose, as needed to process your application.

Form processing and hosting

This website is hosted by a third-party provider, and contact form submissions are processed and stored by that provider so I can read them. Like most websites, the host may automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and performance purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. The page loads a font from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the font is downloaded.

Links to other sites

This site links to my Facebook page and may link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I'm not responsible for how they handle your information.

Children's privacy

This site is meant for adults. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can ask me to update or delete the information you sent through this site at any time by contacting me using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, I'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.

## How keywords are classified

- **venue**: names one of our listings (its full title, or its distinctive part such as "rack city").
- **city**: names a US city of 50,000+ people (Census 2023 estimates) or a state. Ambiguous city names (Mobile, Columbia, Hollywood, Vancouver ...) count only with their state. NYC boroughs count as New York.
- **near_me**: "near me", "nearby", "close to me" without a place.
- **attribute**: a table size, bar box, brand, free pool, leagues (APA, BCA), open late, 24 hour, tournaments or lessons, without a place.
- **other**: everything else, including buying, moving or fixing a table, rules, and swimming pools.

Business names are spotted three ways: words beyond the generic ones ("zanzibar billiards denver"), an "&" ("8 ball sports bar & billiards"), and a place keyword with the same volume as a business search in that city, or with far more searches than the city's "pool hall" keywords ("springfield billiards").

Known limits: a business we do not list that is named after a city ("Brooklyn Billiards" elsewhere) can read as a search in that city when its volume is small; and a city under 50,000 people is not recognized, so its keywords fall to the state or the other classes.

**Subject:** Photos for {Venue}'s page on PlayPoolNation

Hi {Venue} team,

I run PlayPoolNation (playpoolnation.com), a free directory that helps people
find places to play pool. {Venue} is already listed here:
{PlayPoolNation page link}

We'd like the page to show what your place really looks like. Would you send us
a few photos you're happy for us to use? The ones that help players most are:

- the tables and the room
- the outside of the building, so people recognise it
- your logo

Just reply with the photos attached and a quick "OK to use". We'll only use them
on {Venue}'s page and will take them down whenever you ask.

You can also claim the page for free. That lets you add your own photos, hours,
table details, leagues and tournaments yourself, and post events. Use the
"Claim this listing" button on the page.

If anything on the page is wrong, just reply and we'll fix it.

Thanks,
{Your name}
PlayPoolNation
info@playpoolnation.com

How we check facts

Every pool fact on PlayPoolNation has a source you can click and a date it was checked. A person approves each one before it goes live. If we don't know something, we leave it blank.

Where facts come from

  • The venue's own website. Table counts, sizes, brands, prices and specials, read from the venue's pages and menus. We follow each site's robots.txt and go slowly.
  • League operators. League nights come from the operators' own schedules and standings.
  • Owners. Owners who claim a listing can add and correct details. Their facts are marked as owner-supplied.
  • Players. Corrections sent with "Suggest an edit" are checked before they change anything.
  • Maps data. New venues are found through OpenStreetMap and league pages, and we confirm each one is open.

What we never do

  • We don't guess. "Lots of tables" is not a number, so the count stays blank.
  • We don't copy Google Maps or review sites.
  • We don't publish a fact a person hasn't approved.

One exception

When a bar lists pool but no source gives a table size, the page may show 7-foot, the usual bar size, and says so. Any real source replaces it.

Keeping it current

We re-read every source each month and flag changes and closures. Each venue page shows when its facts were last checked. Seen something out of date? Suggest an edit.

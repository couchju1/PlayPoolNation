About

Rack City Billiards is a pool hall at 309 S Bahnson Ave in Sioux Falls, SD. It's open every day. It stays open until 2 AM on Fridays and Saturdays. It has 18 tables. Most are 7-foot, plus two 9-foot tables. They're Diamond and Valley tables. There's darts too.

About

Bangin Ballz Billiards Bar is a pool hall at 9155 S Las Vegas Blvd in Las Vegas, NV. It's open every day. It stays open until 2 AM every night except Sundays. The tables are 7-foot and 9-foot. We don't know how many there are yet. They're Diamond and Rasson tables. You pay for pool by the hour.

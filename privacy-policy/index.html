Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Things you send us: venues, claim requests and edit suggestions, plus an email if you give one so we can follow up.
  • Counts of clicks: when someone taps Call, Directions or Website on a venue, we count it for that venue, with the page, the city, phone or desktop, a broad traffic source (such as "google" or "direct") and the hour. We don't store IP addresses, cookies, user IDs or full referrer addresses with these counts.
  • Your account, if you make one: your email address, a password (stored scrambled by our database provider, never readable by us), and the display name you choose. Your display name and your reviews are public; your email address is not.
  • Wall posts: the name you type and your post, which are public. We don't store your IP address with them.
  • Your location, only if you ask: "Near me" uses your browser's location to sort places. It stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

How we use it

To review and publish submissions, follow up when you asked us to, show venue owners how many people we sent them, and prevent abuse. We do not sell your personal information. We share your email with a venue only when you ask us to, as described below.

Who we share it with

Venues you review, only if you say so. When you write a review there's a box, unticked unless you tick it, to share your email address with that venue so they can contact you about events and offers. If you tick it, we give your email address to that venue (and only that one). You can untick it any time on your account page; a venue that already has your address may keep it, so ask them directly to be removed from their list.

Service providers that run the site: our web host (Hostinger), our database (Supabase), and map tiles from OpenStreetMap-based providers, which load only when you press "Show map".

Cookies and storage

The site doesn't set cookies. If you sign in, your browser keeps your sign-in in its local storage so you stay signed in. Your Pool Passport stamps and Wall name are kept the same way, in your browser only.

Your choices

Delete your account and your reviews any time from your account page. To have anything else you sent us deleted, email info@playpoolnation.com.

Contact: Justin Couch, Sioux Falls, SD, info@playpoolnation.com.
